      - id: what-is-lido-lend
        question: What is Lido Lend?
        answer: >-
          Lido Lend is a set of isolated, on-chain lending markets, with a single collateral asset, administered by the Lido DAO. It is carefully designed for established, long-term looping trades, and to provide both borrowers and lenders with long-term peace of mind.
      - id: how-is-lido-lend-different
        question: How is Lido Lend different from other protocols?
        answer: >-
          Lido Lend introduces a resilient standard for isolated lending markets, built to minimize volatilities and complexities that emerge with advanced borrowing strategies. Specifically, Lido Lend introduces a number of unique features carefully designed to protect users:


          * **Isolated markets:** Isolated, self-contained markets that are built around correlated assets and protected from exposure to generalized market stress.

          * **Stronger lender protection:** Protection against unscreened collateral through the **Guardian Committee**, reducing the risk of tainted assets creating market-wide contagion.

          * **Liquidity under stress:** If loan-asset liquidity is exhausted, **Fallback Exits** provides lenders with a guaranteed exit path into the underlying collateral.

          * **Controlled turbulence:** Defined, in-built user and market protections in case of severe collateral or oracle events.

          * **Mechanism, not discretion:** All security mechanisms - from collateral activation to rate ceilings, Fallback Exits and emergency controls - are transparently verifiable and enforced by code.
      - id: which-markets-are-available
        question: Which markets are available?
        answer: >-
          Lido Lend markets are built exclusively around correlated assets. The first market proposed is WETH against wstETH. Collateral is valued using the wstETH/stETH accounting rate reported by the wstETH contract, with no secondary-market price. Further market expansions will be announced in the near future.
      - id: why-was-lido-lend-created
        question: Why was Lido Lend created?
        answer: >-
          Lido launched in the fall of 2020 with a mission to create a foundational building block for the Ethereum DeFi stack. Whilst the original vision for Lido has been a resounding success, with $20b+ TVL across numerous stakers, premier DeFi integrations and global institutions, recent lending exploits have made clear the need for increasingly narrow lending market structures built to protect both lenders and borrowers alike. This ideology is what underpins Lido Lend.
