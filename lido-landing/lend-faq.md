        question: What is Lido Lend?
        answer: >-
          Lido Lend is a lending protocol governed by the Lido DAO. Each market is isolated and single-purpose, pairing one loan asset with one collateral asset, one oracle, and one exit path. In the first market, lenders supply ETH and borrowers post wstETH as collateral to borrow ETH against it.
