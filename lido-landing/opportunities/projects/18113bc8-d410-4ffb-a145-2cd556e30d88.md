  Wisp ([usewisp.io](http://usewisp.io/)) is a confidential AI harness that lets nobody - even Wisp - read, store or train on your data.\

  Wisp was built on a core idea of privacy by architecture, not by policy. Most AI companies ask you to trust their promises not to read or share your data. We built Wisp so you don’t have to trust us (or anyone) - your prompts are protected by hardware-level encryption and their confidentiality can be verified through Trusted Execution Environment (TEE) attestations.\

  We're early-stage and Wisp’s story is still being written. Your job will be to help tell it loudly, clearly, and every day.


  ### The role


  Although early, the Wisp team has an established marketing strategy, narrative, and positioning. Your job would entail executing on those pillars: you take a two-line brief and turn it into a finished post, thread, script, or video. The output is platform-native, on-brand and on schedule.\

  This is not a junior role: you will receive direction but not micromanagement. We expect you to be self-sufficient and proactive: come back with something better than the brief, push back when an idea is weak, and once you’ve settled into the role and gotten to know the product, bring and execute a steady stream of your own ideas and narrative threads.


  ### Your tasks


  * Turn ideas and briefs into finished content across social platforms: X posts and threads, LinkedIn posts, short-form video scripts and edits, visuals

  * Own the publishing calendar and keep a consistent cadence across X, LinkedIn, Instagram and Youtube/TikTok (more channels as we grow)

  * Edit short-form video: explainers, launch assets, memes. Should be able to use Premiere, CapCut, or your tool of choice

  * Repurpose content: take one strong idea and adapt it natively for different platforms

  * Engage: replying and bringing value into conversations and communities where our audience lives

  * Scan: Keep an active pulse on relevant communities, trending topics, and Wisp-related zeitgeist


  ### Experience


  * 3-6 years of hands-on content creation in tech, AI, web3 or adjacent niches

  * Portfolio of shipped work - posts, threads, videos you can share as reference

  * Content brain with an ear for platform-native voice - you know why an X thread and a LinkedIn post about the same idea should look nothing alike

  * Competent short-form video editor with examples to share

  * Self-managing - you take a brief, ask the right questions and then ship

  * Comfortable with technical subject matter - you don't need to be an engineer, but you should be able to make encryption and confidential computing sound interesting and clear.


  ### Bonus points


  * You've worked in or around privacy, security and/or AI

  * Design skills: Figma, basic motion graphics

  * You've been an early hire at a startup before


  ### What we offer


  * Remote-first, async-friendly

  * Small and agile team - one feedback loop, no committees

  * Full ownership of the execution layer of a brand being built from scratch

  * A front-row seat (+ speaking part) in the AI privacy debate

  * Compensation: USD $60,000-$70,000 (12-month contract)
